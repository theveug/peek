# Peek – Architecture & Design History

Human-facing design rationale and the full history of the visual redesign. This
file is **not** auto-loaded into an agent's context every session (unlike
`CLAUDE.md`) — it's the "how we got here" record, read on demand when a task
actually needs the backstory. The still-active rules and gotchas that must be
respected on *every* change live in `CLAUDE.md`'s "Key conventions"; this file is
the narrative behind them.

See also:
- `CLAUDE.md` — agent operating rules, file map, context-budget rules, active invariants/gotchas.
- `TODO.md` — active backlog only.
- `CHANGELOG.md` — completed work, most-recent first.

## Design principles

- **No info held on a server.** No accounts, no message/chat history persistence,
  no persisted room list. Sessions are in-memory only and disappear the moment the
  last peer leaves (`SessionManager.js`). Media is P2P wherever possible; even when
  a TURN relay is needed as fallback, it's blind-relaying encrypted bytes, not
  storing or inspecting anything.
- When weighing a new feature, check it against this first. Features that require
  persistent server-side storage (accounts, saved channel lists, chat history) are
  a deliberate exception, not a default — flag the tradeoff explicitly rather than
  building it in quietly.
- **No fake controls.** Don't ship a toggle/button that does nothing to match a
  mockup. Where the mockup drew interactive switches for things that are already
  always-true and non-configurable (P2P mesh, in-memory only, blind signalling),
  the app shows read-only info cards instead — a fake toggle is actively
  misleading. Same reasoning retired the mockup's "Zoom" button (no action to wire
  it to) and kept it wheel-only.

## Scaling ceiling & regulatory considerations (SFU migration, 2026-07-09)

*Not legal advice — this is engineering-team reasoning about the tradeoff, captured so
the SFU decision in `TODO.md` isn't made without this context. Get actual
jurisdiction-specific legal review before committing to an SFU design, especially if
transcoding/mixing/recording is ever added on top of it.*

The mesh architecture's scaling ceiling (see `TODO.md`'s "Scaling ceiling" section) is
real — full mesh is N×(N-1) connections, and quality tiers (`_qualityTier()`) only
shrink each connection's bitrate, they don't reduce the connection *count*. The actual
fix at large room sizes is an SFU (Selective Forwarding Unit): one upload per client,
fanned out server-side. That's a deliberate architectural shift away from "no info held
on a server," so before picking it up, it's worth being precise about what legal
exposure it would and wouldn't add — a concern raised directly by the user.

- **It's very unlikely to trigger age-verification/ID-check laws** (e.g. Australia's
  social-media minimum-age rules, in force since ~Dec 2025). Those regimes key off
  *product features* — persistent accounts, a social graph, content
  discovery/search across users, ongoing feed posting — not transport architecture.
  Most explicitly carve out services whose sole/primary purpose is messaging or
  voice/video calling. Zoom and Teams run centralized SFUs and aren't captured by
  these laws; the carve-out is about *what the product is*, not *how media moves*.
  Peek's no-accounts, ephemeral-room design is what keeps it on the "communication
  tool" side of that line, and an SFU migration wouldn't change that classification
  by itself — only adding social features (profiles, directories, discovery, feeds)
  would.
- **The real legal risk is different: carriage-vs-hosting classification.** Today's
  TURN relay (fallback only) is a blind byte-forwarder — it relays DTLS-SRTP
  encrypted packets without ever holding decryption keys, which is legally similar to
  a telecom carrier ("mere conduit" under the EU's e-Commerce Directive/DSA,
  common-carrier treatment under Australia's Telecommunications Act): no technical
  capability to inspect content, so no reasonable expectation to moderate it. A pure
  forwarding SFU (just picking which simulcast layer to relay per receiver) can
  preserve this property, especially combined with client-side E2EE via WebRTC
  insertable streams, so the server never holds keys. But an SFU that needs to
  **transcode, mix, or compose** streams (audio mixing for large calls, recording,
  server-side composition) typically has to decrypt server-side, even with nothing
  ever written to disk — and that's the point regulators/plaintiffs can run a
  "capability implies duty" argument: once you *can* inspect content, even
  transiently, you can be expected to. Australia's Online Safety Act industry codes
  around proactive CSAM detection are the concrete version of this, and they're
  framed around *reasonable technical capability*, not storage.
- **Keep privacy-law exposure and carriage-classification exposure separate** — they
  don't move together. Privacy-law exposure (GDPR-style, Australian Privacy Act) is
  about *storage* and stays minimal under a stateless SFU exactly as it does under
  the current mesh, as long as nothing persists server-side. Carriage/hosting
  classification is about *capability to inspect decrypted content*, and only shifts
  if a future SFU design needs to decrypt for transcoding/mixing/recording.
- **If/when the SFU migration is scoped**: favor a pure forwarding design (no
  transcode/mix/record) with client-side E2EE via insertable streams so the server
  never holds keys, and keep logging ephemeral/connection-metadata-only — that
  preserves the current legal posture. Recording or server-side composition
  features, if ever wanted, are the things that would actually need a fresh legal
  review, not the SFU migration on its own.

## Visual redesign (Phases 0-5 — all complete)

Based on a Claude Design handoff pack (`peek-design.zip`, originally from Downloads — `README.md` + `Peek.dc.html` prototype; the prototype is inline-styled preview-runtime HTML, **not** meant to be copied verbatim, just a spec for appearance/behavior). Phased: 0 decisions → **1 tokens/fonts (done)** → **2 screen re-skins — all sub-phases done (2a lobby, 2b top bar/Invite popover, 2c members sidebar, 2d stage/controls dock, 2e chat/files/polls panel)** → **3 settings panel (done)** → **4 removed the legacy glass/gradient background (done)** → **5 Background tint picker (done)**. DMs are explicitly out of scope (was a stray future-idea note in the pack, not a real ask). Phases 4-5 were new scope beyond the original pack — nothing further currently planned.

- **Phase 1 tokens**: `tailwind.css`'s `:root`/`body.light` now have a second, *additive* token set (`--bg1`..`--bg5`, `--border`, `--input`, `--t1`..`--t3`, `--accent`/`--accentH`/`--accentSoft`/`--accentSoft2`/`--accentText`, `--green`/`--amber`/`--red`) alongside the original `--color-*`/`--surface-*` tokens — original markup keeps working unchanged; only new surfaces (currently just the settings overlay) are built on the new set. Accent tokens are runtime-only, owned entirely by `AccentManager.js` (static token file values are just the pre-JS default/no-FOUC fallback, Violet hue 286).
- **Fonts**: self-hosted Public Sans + Fira Code (single variable-weight `.woff2` each, covers all needed weights) + Material Symbols Rounded (`public/assets/fonts.css`, files in `public/assets/fonts/`). `html, body` now default to Public Sans; `.font-mono-app` utility class and `.chat-markdown code` use Fira Code — **most of the app still hasn't been swept to actually use `.font-mono-app`** for room codes/latency/etc. Material Symbols is the *unsubsetted* full icon set (~5.3 MB, one-time cached download) — subsetting to just the ~30 icons actually used is a worthwhile follow-up, not done yet.
- **Phase 3 settings panel**: `#settings-modal` went from a small centered modal (the original bug report: too many fields, no scroll region, clipped on short viewports) to a full-frame overlay — 262px left nav (5 sections) + a content column with its own `overflow-y-auto`. Owned by the new `public/client/SettingsPanel.js`, same `#settings-modal` id/open-close contract so nothing else had to change. **Every field applies + persists immediately on change now — there's no Save/Cancel step anymore** (matches the design spec's interaction model; deliberate behavior change from the old form-submit pattern). Nickname change fires on `change` (blur), not `input`, so it doesn't broadcast a nickname update per keystroke.
  - **One settings surface everywhere**: the standalone `/settings` page (`settings.html`/`settings.js`) was retired — it was already dead (nothing linked to it) and out of sync (missing several fields). `lobby.html` now carries its own copy of the settings-overlay markup + a gear button, and instantiates `SettingsPanel` with no `ui`/`peerManager` (both optional in the constructor) so pre-room settings still load/persist to `localStorage` without erroring on the parts that need a live connection (status picker, live quality re-apply, nickname broadcast — all guarded with `?.`).
  - **Privacy & P2P section deliberately deviates from the mockup**: it shows read-only info cards (not interactive toggles) for things that are already always-true and non-configurable (P2P mesh, in-memory only, signalling server blind to content) — the mockup draws these as toggle switches, but shipping fake toggles that do nothing would be actively misleading. The one *real* toggle there is `autoAcceptFiles`.
  - **Gotcha already hit**: the custom `.settings-switch` toggle (checkbox + a `.settings-switch-track` sibling span for the visual pill/thumb) needs `pointer-events: none` on the track — without it, the visually-on-top track span intercepts every click meant for the checkbox underneath, so the toggle looks clickable but silently does nothing. Caught by `tests/file-consent.mjs` failing to check the box, not by eyeballing it.
- **Quick room settings popover (2026-07-04, follow-up to Phase 3)**: not every setting deserves a trip through the 5-section full panel — status, `autoAcceptFiles`, `followActiveSpeaker`, and screen/cam quality are things people plausibly want to flip mid-room, so they also live in a lightweight popover off `#quick-settings-button` (`public/client/QuickRoomSettings.js`). It's a second UI surface only — same `localStorage` keys and `PeerManager` methods as `SettingsPanel.js`, no duplicated data/validation logic, and its "All settings" link hands off to the real `SettingsPanel.open()`. The status control changed shape here too: the old inline 3-button segmented picker (still what `SettingsPanel.js`'s Profile section shows) vs. the popover's `#quick-status-trigger`/`#quick-status-menu` click-to-open dropdown — same `peerManager.setManualStatus()` underneath either way. Screen/cam quality in the popover only exposes resolution (not fps) to keep it compact.
- **Phase 2a lobby re-skin (2026-07-04)**: `public/lobby.html` rebuilt from a small `max-w-md` card onto Phase 1 tokens — a 900px-wide column: hero (squircle accent icon + "Start a room" + subcopy, blurred `--accentSoft` radial glow behind it via `.lobby-glow`), a 2-col `.lobby-card` grid (Create/Join), the Saved Rooms list re-skinned into a `.lobby-recent-grid` of cards, and a static 4-item privacy footer. New CSS lives in tailwind.css's `/* ===== Lobby (redesign, Phase 2a) ===== */` section.
  - **Stepper replaces the max-participants `<select>`**: `#create-max-peers` is now a readonly `<input type="number">` (min 2, max 12, default 6) driven by `#create-max-peers-dec`/`-inc` buttons — same id/value contract as before, so `#create-form`'s submit handler didn't need to change.
  - **Join input gained paste-link support**: a `paste` handler takes the pasted text, strips non-alphanumeric, and keeps the trailing 5 characters — so pasting a full invite link (`https://peek.app/r/AB12C`) extracts the room code instead of concatenating the whole URL.
  - **Recent room cards**: `savedRooms.js`'s `saveRoom()` stamps a `savedAt: Date.now()` on every upsert; `lobby.html` calls `saveRoom()` again on a successful "Rejoin" click so lastActive stays current. Each card's avatar hue is computed client-side from the room's code+label via a string hash (`hueFromString()`) — deterministic per room, nothing persisted.
- **Phase 2b top bar + Invite popover (2026-07-04)**: the room page had **no persistent top chrome at all** before this — just a floating `#room-header` pill plus the hover-reveal bottom `#controls` bar. User chose the mockup's literal always-visible 58px bar over an enhanced floating-pill alternative. New `.topbar` row inserted as the first child of the root `flex flex-col h-screen` wrapper (`public/index.html`), so the existing `flex-grow` row below it just shrinks via flexbox.
  - **Left**: brand + room-state pill (`#topbar-room-name`/`#topbar-room-lock`/`#topbar-room-code`, populated via `UIController.setRoomMeta()` from `App.js`'s `'init'` handler). **Right**: peer-count+latency pill (`#topbar-peer-count`/`-cap`, `#topbar-latency`), the Invite popover, the **relocated** `#settings-button`/`#quick-settings-button` (moved out of the bottom controls dock — pure DOM relocation, same ids), and an identity pill (`#topbar-identity-*`).
  - **`#share-button`'s old silent instant-clipboard-copy behavior was removed entirely** — replaced by `InvitePopover.js`, which shows the room code and a direct link with their own Copy buttons and a toast for feedback.
  - **Mesh latency is a new data point**: `PeerManager._pollConnectionStats()` already fetched per-peer RTT every 3s but discarded the raw ms; now it accumulates valid `rtt * 1000` samples and calls `UIController.updateMeshLatency(avgMs)` once per poll tick — reuses the existing timer.
  - **Server plumbing**: `WebSocketServer.js`'s `'init'` payload now includes `hasPassword`/`maxPeers` (already returned by `getSessionMeta()`) so the client can render the lock icon and cap without a new endpoint.
  - **Cascade-layers gotcha (origin of the standing rule in CLAUDE.md)**: toggling a `.material-symbols-rounded` icon's visibility via the Tailwind `.hidden` utility silently didn't work — Tailwind v4 wraps utilities in CSS `@layer`s, and any unlayered rule beats any layered rule regardless of specificity/order; `fonts.css`'s `.material-symbols-rounded` is unlayered, so it always won over `.hidden`'s layered `display: none`. Fixed by toggling `element.style.display` directly.
- **Phase 2c members sidebar (2026-07-04)**: `#members-sidebar`/`_createParticipantCard()` re-skinned onto Phase 1 tokens — square (11px radius) avatars with a deterministic per-peer hue (`UIController._hueFromString()`, same algorithm as `lobby.html`'s but duplicated locally since the two files are separate unbundled entry points), a compact right-side column (signal bars over mic icon, reusing existing `_signalBarsSvg()`/`_micOnSvg()`/`_micOffSvg()`), and a static "Fully meshed" trust card.
  - **Self card moved out of the visible list into a new footer strip** (avatar/nickname/status dropdown/settings gear) — explicit user choice. **The self card is still created in the DOM** (`addSelf()` unchanged) so the existing peerId-keyed update methods keep working unmodified — only visually hidden via `.members-panel .participant-card[data-self="1"] { display: none; }`. Removing it from the DOM entirely would have broken those methods' `!el → addParticipant(peerId)` fallback paths.
  - **Member rows have no hover/click affordance** — the mockup's rows open a private DM, but DMs are out of scope, so `.members-panel .participant-card:hover { background: transparent; }` overrides the shared hover rule.
  - **New `public/client/SidebarIdentity.js`** wires the footer's status dropdown + settings gear — mirrors `QuickRoomSettings.js`'s pattern under new ids so it doesn't collide with the top bar's identical-shaped dropdown. Deliberate second functional entry point, not a redundant copy — the top bar's identity display is glance-only, the sidebar footer adds real click-to-change-status.
  - **Mobile gotcha (origin of the standing rule in CLAUDE.md)**: the footer strip rendered underneath the fixed bottom `#controls` bar (`z-index: 90` vs the drawer's `80`). Fixed with `padding-bottom: calc(11px + 64px + env(safe-area-inset-bottom))` on `.sidebar-identity` in the `@media (max-width: 767px)` block, not a z-index change.
- **Phase 2d stage + controls dock (2026-07-04)**: re-skinned the grid/focus stage and the bottom controls dock. Scoped down via two explicit user decisions: no focus-view filmstrip (focus view is single-stream only today) and no "React" floating-emoji button (doesn't exist).
  - **New stage header row** (`#stage-header`, first child of `#videos`): a Grid/Focus segmented control (`#stage-view-grid`/`#stage-view-focus`, calling `ui.setViewMode()` directly), an active-stream count (`watchedTiles.size / maxWatchedTiles`), and a quality readout that only appears while screen-sharing (`PeerManager.getScreenQualityLabel()`, pushed via `ui.updateStageQuality()` from `startSharing()`/`stopSharing()`).
  - **Grid tiles** (`_buildGridCell()`): rounded corners, a mic icon in the name label (new `this._micEnabled` map keyed by real peerId, updated in `updateParticipantMic()`'s existing choke point), a screen-share resolution badge from the `<video>`'s own `videoWidth`/`videoHeight` on `loadedmetadata`, and the speaking ring (`setSpeaking()` now toggles `.status-talking` on any grid tile matching `peerId` or `peerId-cam`, not just the participant-list avatar).
  - **Focus view**: a "`{name}` is sharing" pill and quality caption, populated in `focusStream()`/`_updateFocusMeta()` from existing data. Zoom/pan stays wheel-only.
  - **Controls dock**: circular pill buttons (`.dock-btn`, 48px); the Leave button becomes a wide labeled red pill. **`#grid-toggle`/`#grid-button` removed** (superseded by the stage-header toggle). **`#theme-toggle` relocated into the top bar** (`.topbar-icon-btn`, same id/handler). A mic-mode badge (`#mic-mode-badge`, "PTT"/"PTM") surfaces `localStorage.micMode` via `ui.updateMicModeBadge()`.
  - **Cascade-layers gotcha hit again**: `updateShareButton()` toggled `#stop-share-button` via `.hidden`, which broke once its markup switched to the custom unlayered `.dock-btn` class (which sets `display: flex`). Fixed with inline `style.display` — this is the second instance that established the standing rule now in CLAUDE.md.
- **Phase 2e chat/files/polls panel (2026-07-04) — Phase 2 complete.** Nothing here was a new feature — reactions, replies, code-block copy, file cards, and poll voting bars already existed; this was a `bg-indigo-*`/`border-indigo-500` → design-token sweep plus a few component restyles. **Polls stay an in-chat card + modal**, not promoted to a third tab like the mockup (an IA change, not a re-skin).
  - **Tab bar** (`#tab-chat`/`#tab-files`): old underline tabs replaced with a pill-segmented control (`.panel-tabs`/`.panel-tab-btn.active`). `UIController._switchTab()` simplified from 8 conditional class toggles to one `.active` toggle each.
  - **Chat composer** (`#chat-input`): rebuilt into a rounded pill (`.chat-composer`) with `--bg5` circular icon buttons. **Added a dedicated send button** (`#send-message-btn`, wired to the same `sendMessage()` Enter already called) — the app previously had no visible send affordance at all, a real mobile-usability gap.
  - **Files tab**: added a persistent "Click to send a file" dropzone card (`#files-dropzone-card`) wired to the existing hidden `#file-input`. Note a pre-existing gap flagged here: drag-and-drop and paste are wired at the Chat tab's DOM level and don't fire while the Files tab is showing. Picking a file from this card calls `ui._switchTab('chat')` so the user sees what they staged.
  - **Chat avatars**: `.chat-avatar` `border-radius` changed from circle to `6px`. The per-name color algorithm (`ChatUI._colorForName()`) was left unchanged.
  - **Poll modal + option bars**: `focus:ring-indigo-500/50` replaced with a `.poll-input:focus` rule; option bars swapped hardcoded indigo utilities for `--accentSoft`/`--accent` via `.poll-option-voted`/`.poll-option-fill`/`.poll-option-hoverable`.
- **Phase 4 — removed the legacy glass/gradient background (2026-07-05).** With Phase 2 done, the chat panel, members sidebar, controls dock, panel-toggle tabs, and update banner were still using the pre-redesign glass-morphism look — `backdrop-filter: blur()` over the animated gradient canvas (`GradientBackground.js` + `#gradient-canvas`). Confirmed via AskUserQuestion: remove entirely to match the mockup's flat solid panels.
  - **Deleted `public/client/GradientBackground.js`** and its two call sites (`App.js`, `lobby.html`) plus the `<canvas id="gradient-canvas">` from both pages.
  - **Flattened every glass surface** onto solid tokens: `.chat-panel`/`.members-panel` → `background: var(--bg2)`; `.dock-bar`/`.panel-tab` → `background: var(--bg3); border: 1px solid var(--border)`; update banner likewise. Removed the dead `.surface-glass`/`.controls-glass` classes, `--surface-glass`/`--controls-glass`/`--controls-border`/`--gradient-1..4` tokens, the `#gradient-canvas` rule, and two orphaned selectors.
  - **Real bug found during verification**: the canvas had quietly been the page's *only* source of a background color (`body`/`html`/`.lobby-page` never declared one), so removing it left plain white showing through the lobby and stage. Fixed with `background: var(--bg1)` on the base `html, body` rule. Any future removal of a full-page background element should check whether anything else declares a base page color.
- **Phase 5 — Background tint picker (2026-07-05), new scope from an earlier "Mesh" design prototype (pre-dates the repo, shown via screenshot).** Adds a 6-swatch "Background Tint" picker to Settings → Appearance (Violet/Slate/Plum/Forest/Sand/Ocean), between Accent Color and Preview — shifts the neutral `--bg1..5`/`--border`/`--input` scale's hue independently of the accent.   - **Real bug found during verification (origin of the standing rule in CLAUDE.md)**: the first implementation applied the override on `documentElement.style` (matching `AccentManager.js`) and silently didn't work while light theme was active — `--bg1..5`/`--border`/`--input` are the first redesign tokens redeclared per-theme (`body.light`), unlike accent tokens which only live in `:root`, and a directly-matched declaration on `<body>` beats an inherited value from `<html>`. Fixed by overriding on `document.body.style` instead.

## Feature narratives moved out of CLAUDE.md's Key Conventions (2026-07-15 housekeeping pass)

These were originally full entries in CLAUDE.md's "Key conventions." Each has been reduced there to a one-line pointer (the still-load-bearing rule, if any) referencing this section — moved here verbatim rather than trimmed, since the point of the split is that this file carries the "how we got here" story and CLAUDE.md only carries the standing rule.

- **Draggable self-view PiPs (2026-07-07)**: `#self-view`/`#self-cam-view` (your own screen/cam preview, `UIController.addStream()`) are drag-to-reposition (Discord-style) via `UIController._makeDraggable(el, storageKey)`, using Pointer Events (`pointerdown`/`pointermove`/`pointerup` + `setPointerCapture`) so mouse and touch share one code path. Position is a personal layout preference, not session state, so it's persisted to `localStorage['pipPosition:self-view']`/`['pipPosition:self-cam-view']` and restored on the next stream/reload — unlike `peerVolumes`, there's no peerId-reassignment problem here since the storage key is the PiP's fixed role (`self-view`/`self-cam-view`), not a peerId. A dragged element gets `dataset.dragged = 'true'`, which `_updateSelfViewPositions()` (the existing right-edge auto-stacking for when both screen+cam PiPs are visible at once) checks and skips — otherwise every stream add/remove would snap a manually-placed PiP back to the corner. `window.resize` reclamps any dragged PiP back on-screen (`_reclampDraggedPip`) in case a saved position from a larger window is now off-screen.
- **Top-bar mesh signal icon (2026-07-08)**: the peer-count pill's old "Xms · mesh" text is now the same colored signal-bars icon used per-participant in the members list (`UIController._signalBarsSvg`/`.participant-signal-icon`) — `UIController.updateMeshSignal(ms, tier)` (renamed from `updateMeshLatency`, `#topbar-signal-wrap`/`#topbar-signal-icon`), with the exact ms + quality label only as a hover tooltip. `tier` is the *worst* per-peer connection quality across the mesh (`PeerManager._pollConnectionStats`'s `_qualityRank` ranking), not an average — one bad connection among several good ones should still show as bad, not get diluted out.
- **Grid/focus tile overlay cleanup (2026-07-08)**: the grid tile's "Stop watching" control used to be a text pill at `top-right`, directly on top of the screen-share resolution badge (`.grid-tile-badge`, also `top-right`) — the two visibly overlapped. Fixed by giving each corner exactly one thing: `.grid-tile-stop-btn` (icon-only, red `close` glyph, matches `--red`/hover-fills-red like `.participant-mod-kick`) owns top-right, `.grid-tile-badge` moved to bottom-right, `.grid-tile-name` (mic icon + nickname) stays bottom-left but now has the same dark pill background as `.focus-quality-caption` instead of a bare text-shadow, so both views read as one consistent label style. Focus view previously showed the peer's name **twice** — an accent-colored `.focus-sharing-pill` banner top-left ("● Name is sharing") *and* a separate `.focus-quality-caption` pill bottom-left ("Name · WxH") — collapsed into one bottom-left pill (`#focus-quality-caption` now holds both the live dot and `#focus-caption-text`); `_updateFocusMeta()` targets `#focus-caption-text` instead of setting `caption.textContent` directly (that would have wiped out the dot span). Focus view also gained the same stop-watching affordance grid tiles already had — `#focus-stop-button`, grouped with the existing PiP button in `#focus-icon-btn-group` (`.focus-icon-btn`s are no longer individually `position:absolute`; the group wrapper owns that now, so multiple icon buttons stack horizontally instead of colliding). Clicking it calls `_unwatchTile(focusedPeerId)` **without** clearing `focusedPeerId` or falling through to `updateLayout()`'s auto-refocus branch — in a 2-person room `remoteStreams[0]` would still resolve to the same peer, so an unconditional refocus would instantly re-watch the very stream you just paused. Instead `_renderFocusWatchState()` toggles a dedicated `#focus-paused-overlay` (reuses the exact grid-tile paused-overlay classes/copy — "Click to watch · stream paused · saving bandwidth" — for one consistent paused look everywhere) over the video, hiding the caption/icon-buttons until the overlay itself is clicked to resume (`_watchTile` + restore `srcObject`). Called from `focusStream()`, the stop/resume handlers, and every `updateLayout()` focus-branch pass, so it can't drift out of sync with `watchedTiles`.
- **"Me" sentinel removed (2026-07-13)**: the local echo of chat/poll/file messages used to pass the literal string `'Me'` as `sender` — displayed as-is, so your own messages showed "Me" instead of your actual nickname regardless of what you'd set. Every self-echo call site now passes the resolved nickname (`localStorage.getItem('nickname') || 'Anonymous'`) instead, while self-vs-remote detection moved fully to the already-existing explicit `isSelf` boolean param (added to `addPollMessage`/`addFileMessage`, which previously inferred it by comparing `sender === 'Me'` — a latent bug, since a remote peer naming themselves "Me" would have false-positived as self). `_replyQuoteHtml()`'s self-color check compares against the current nickname the same way. This also fixed `getMessageHistory()`'s recap transcript, which was showing "Me" for the same reason.

Without the resend-on-join behavior, the room's only peer having their socket drop and reconnect (or even just refreshing the page) while nobody else is in the room deletes-then-lazily-recreates the session via `SessionManager.removePeer()`'s immediate-delete-when-empty behavior, which would otherwise silently lose the topic; `SessionManager.addPeer()`'s lazy-create path adopts the resent `topic` the exact same way it already adopts a resent `password`. `UIController.setTopic()` is the single live-update chokepoint (mirrors `setHasPassword()`'s "touches only its own DOM, no side effects" shape) — also derives the creator-only edit-pencil visibility and shows the banner (with just the pencil, no text) to the creator even when no topic is set yet, so there's a way to add one; re-derived from `updateModeratorStatus()` too, since creator status can change.
- **Local call recording, full mechanism (2026-08-26)**: a dock "Record" button + mode-picker popover (`#record-options-popover`, the standard `wireQuickPopover()`/`wireSegmentedPicker()` pattern). `PeerManager.isRecordingAllowed(peerId)` defaults to allowed until a peer explicitly opts out. While an 'audio'/'composite' recording is active, `recording-status` broadcasts a card badge (`.participant-recording-icon`, `UIController.setPeerRecording()`) + inline system message to every other peer — mirroring the existing "everyone sees who has moderator powers" transparency stance; 'local' mode never broadcasts this since it captures nobody else. - **@mention autocomplete, full design (2026-08-31, `MentionAutocomplete.js`, owner-reported)**: since `_processMentions()` only ever highlights an `@` immediately followed by one of the room's *exact* current nicknames, typing one by hand (nicknames can contain spaces/unicode and up to 60 chars) was error-prone with no help — this closes that gap with a Discord-style popover anchored at the live caret, not a button-triggered menu (the owner explicitly didn't want another "click first" step like the composer's "+" emoji menu). Query detection (`findMentionQuery()`) looks for an `@` that starts the text or follows whitespace, then treats everything up to the caret as the live query — deliberately *not* stopped at the next space, since a query needs to be able to contain spaces itself to match a multi-word nickname; it only closes once nothing in the participant list matches, or on a newline/length cap. Caret pixel position has no textarea API, so `composerCaret.js`'s `caretRect()` uses the standard hidden "mirror div" technique (a styled off-screen div holding the pre-caret text plus a marker span) — `MIRRORED_PROPS` only copies properties that affect text layout/wrapping, since the mirror is never actually shown. Picking a suggestion (`pick()`) splices in `@name ` and dispatches a real `input` event so `App.js`'s own auto-grow/typing-status listener reacts exactly like a manual edit would, guarded by a one-shot `suppressNextInput` flag so that synthetic event doesn't immediately re-open the popover (the inserted text can itself still substring-match a longer nickname).
- **Autocomplete hover bug, full narrative (2026-08-31 fix, owner-reported: "I can hover items but can't click them, have to use keyboard Enter to select")**: both `MentionAutocomplete.js` and `EmojiAutocomplete.js` originally called their `renderList()` (which does `listEl.innerHTML = ''` then recreates every item) from each item's own `mouseenter` handler, just to toggle which one had the `.active` class. Replacing the DOM node currently under the mouse pointer — even with a visually identical replacement at the same screen position, with the mouse not having moved at all — makes the browser re-hit-test and fire a **new** `mouseenter` on the replacement node, which called `renderList()` again, which replaced it again... a self-sustaining loop that kept swapping the hovered item out from under the cursor before a real click's mousedown/mouseup pair could ever land on one stable element (reproduced via Playwright as "element was detached from the DOM, retrying" on every click attempt). Fixed by splitting the two concerns: `renderList()` now only ever runs when the **match set itself** changes (from `open()`, after a `refresh()`/`applyMatches()`), and a new `setActive(i)` — a plain `classList.remove('active')`/`add('active')` toggle against an `itemEls` array kept in sync by `renderList()` — is what hover (`mouseenter`) and keyboard arrow-nav now call instead.
- **:emoji autocomplete, full design (2026-08-31, `EmojiAutocomplete.js`, companion to the entry above)**: same caret-anchored-popover shape as `@mention` autocomplete (shares its caret math via `composerCaret.js`), triggered by `:` instead of `@`. **No canonical `:smile:`-style shortcode table exists in this app** — `EmojiPicker.js`'s vendored dataset only carries a plain-English `n` annotation ("grinning face") plus a `t` tags array, not gemoji-style short names — so rather than vendor a second dataset just for exact names, `findMatches()` fuzzy-matches the query against that same annotation/tags data (identical matching to `EmojiPicker.js`'s own search box). Shares `EmojiPicker.js`'s lazily-loaded dataset via its re-exported `ensureEmojiData()`/`getRecentEmoji()`/`recordRecentEmoji()` (added to that file the same day) rather than fetching/caching a second independent copy — picking a suggestion also calls `recordRecentEmoji()` so recency stays in sync with the click-to-open picker. Before the dataset has ever loaded (first `:` typed on a fresh page load, if the click-to-open picker hasn't been opened first), the popover briefly shows a "Loading…" placeholder — `loadGeneration` guards that in-flight load against a later keystroke (or dismissal) superseding it before it resolves. The two triggers are wired independently and don't coordinate with each other — each only acts while its own popover is open, and each trigger character's own "must follow whitespace" guard means a realistic overlap (e.g. typing a mention nickname that itself contains a colon) essentially never produces two popovers open at once in practice; this wasn't engineered around further since the worst case is a rare, purely cosmetic double-popover, not a functional break.
- **Message edit/delete, full UI mechanism (2026-07-11)**: `ChatUI._messageMeta` (`Map<messageId, {isSelf, rawText}>`, capped at 300) powers the hover-bar Edit/Delete buttons (only rendered when `isSelf`), the inline edit textarea (`_beginEdit`: Enter saves, Escape cancels), and the Copy button (which reads current `rawText` from the map, not its stale closure param). `applyChatEdit()` appends a `.chat-edited-tag` "(edited)" after the timestamp; `applyChatDelete()` removes the bubble outright — no tombstone, and reply-quotes keep their snapshot text (their click-to-scroll just no-ops). Delete uses a two-step armed confirm on the button itself (tooltip flips via `data-tip`, reverts after 3s) — same pattern as Settings' clear-data.
- **Clear all local data, full UI/confirm-pattern detail (2026-07-06)**: `#settings-clear-data` in Settings' Privacy & P2P section (present in both `index.html` and `lobby.html`'s separate copy of the settings markup, wired once in the shared `SettingsPanel._wirePrivacy()`). No confirm-before-destructive-action pattern existed anywhere else in the app to reuse (removing a saved room and kicking a peer both fire immediately, no confirmation) — this introduces a two-step "click again to confirm" button (reverts after 4s if not confirmed) rather than a native `confirm()`, to stay in the app's own visual language. Worth knowing if you're the one testing it: this also wipes `nickname` and `peek.savedRooms` (including saved rooms' plaintext passwords), not just cosmetic prefs — `App.js` regenerates a fresh random nickname on the reload that follows, it does not come back empty.
- **Local chat history retention, mechanism detail (2026-07-24, opt-in)**: `ChatUI.js`'s `addChatMessage()`/`ensureFileGroup()` (caption text only, never file entries) persist via `appendMessage()`, `applyChatEdit()`/`applyChatDelete()` call `updateMessage()`/`deleteMessage()` so persisted history can't drift from what's actually still true. Loaded history renders through the same `addChatMessage()` path as live messages but with `isHistorical=true`: muted (`.chat-message-history`), no hover action bar (Edit/Delete/reply/emoji-picker — interacting with a days-old message as if live means nothing once the original peers are gone), and a one-time `.chat-history-divider` marks where live traffic starts. Reactions aren't persisted or restored; no per-room enable/disable, one global toggle like `micThreshold`/`autoAcceptFiles`.
